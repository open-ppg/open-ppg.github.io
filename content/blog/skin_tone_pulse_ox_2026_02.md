[Recent research](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2025-085535) by Daniel Martin and colleagues, published in The BMJ, examined how skin tone affects the accuracy of pulse oximeters used in the NHS COVID Oximetry @home programme.

By comparing readings from commonly used pulse oximeters with gold-standard arterial blood gas measurements, the study found that the pulse oximeters overestimated blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂) when the true oxygen saturation was low, and underestimated SpO2 when the true oxygen saturation was high. The authors conclude that this "could translate into potentially clinically important differences in false negative and false positive rates for detecting hypoxaemia".
