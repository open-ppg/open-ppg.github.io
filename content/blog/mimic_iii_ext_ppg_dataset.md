A [new dataset](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iii-ext-ppg/1.0.0/), named MIMIC-III-Ext-PPG, has recently been released. It is a benchmark dataset designed for developing and testing PPG models and algorithms. The dataset contains 4.9 million 30-second segments of PPG signals, alongside heart rhythm labels and reference parameters such as heart rate, blood pressure, and respiratory rate. The data were extracted from the larger MIMIC-III dataset, which was collected from critically-ill patients in hospital. It also contains other simultaneous physiological signals, such as ECG and invasive blood pressure signals.

The dataset is available [here](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iii-ext-ppg/1.0.0/).
