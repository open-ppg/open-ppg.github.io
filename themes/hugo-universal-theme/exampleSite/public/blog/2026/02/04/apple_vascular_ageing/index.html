A PPG-derived aging clock

By Peter H Charlton February 4, 2026

Researchers at Apple developed a method to predict age from Apple Watch PPG signals. Key findings included:

  • The model could predict age accurately.
  • The difference between predicted age and actual age was associated with current diseases, future diseases, smoking and exercise habits, and more.
  • Predicted age changed over time during pregnancy.
